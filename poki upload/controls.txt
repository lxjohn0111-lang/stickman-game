Keyboard and mouse
- W A S D: move
- Mouse: look
- Left click: fire (hold for automatic weapons)
- Right click: steady aim (hold)
- Space: jump; let go of a ladder
- Shift: sprint
- C or Left Ctrl: crouch (hold)
- F: open doors, use switches and consoles, swap guns, take ammo
- R: reload
- 1 / 2, Q or mouse wheel: main weapon / sidearm
- V: switch visual style (Classic / Neobrutalist)
- Esc: pause

Touch (phones and tablets, landscape)
- Left thumb: drag to move (push to the edge to sprint)
- Right thumb: drag to look
- Fire button: shoot (hold for automatic weapons); light aim assist while firing
- Aim, jump, crouch, reload, weapon switch and pause buttons
- Tap the prompt under the crosshair to open doors, use switches and swap guns
